Our first meeting was a serendipitous collision of paths within the bustling halls of our college campus. Perhaps it was during orientation week, amidst the flurry of new faces and nervous excitement, or in the quiet corner of a crowded library, where our eyes first locked in a moment of shared curiosity. From that instant, an unspoken connection sparked between us, as if the universe had conspired to bring two kindred spirits together. Oct 30 2019

Our first date was a magical blend of nervous anticipation and effortless connection, set against the backdrop of a cozy café or a scenic spot that sparked conversation and laughter.
